# 07 — New business requirements gap analysis

The requested behavior is a proposal. Existing dispatch, geography and persistence are traced in [05](05-realtime-dispatch-and-payments.md) and [02](02-backend-and-database.md).

| Priority / severity | Confirmed current gap and source | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 / High | OTP column migration 0015 is absent from the journal. See [02](02-backend-and-database.md). | Audit deployed and fresh schemas, then plan a forward migration and upgrade tests before relying on consistent pickup/completion behavior. |
| 2 / Critical | Order-level locking does not serialize capacity across orders. `backend/src/riders/rider-assignment.service.ts:182–219,280–315`; independent claim/accept paths at `backend/src/riders/rider-offers.service.ts:320,567`. | Lock or atomically reserve a slot on the rider in the same transaction that owns the order/delivery, then recheck eligibility and committed load. Use one documented lock order across all assignment entry points and retry transient conflicts. Roll back the slot if the delivery write fails. Release exactly once on cancellation, terminal completion or reassignment; reconcile existing counters during rollout. Test two orders competing for one slot and repeated delivery events. |
| 3 / High | Existing limits are straight-line and split among discovery, checkout, assignment and pool/claim (see [05](05-realtime-dispatch-and-payments.md)). No shared admin road-distance policy is wired through those checks. | Add versioned admin policy and a server routing adapter. Use a bounding box/Haversine only for coarse pruning when safe, then measure outlet→customer and rider→outlet separately. Keep units, routing profile, freshness, cache expiry and provider failures explicit. Evaluate discovery, checkout, dispatch and claim consistently and persist policy version, distance source and measurement on decisions. Keep payout distance semantics explicit. |
| 4 / High | Current assignment generates an offer and awaits rider acceptance. Capacity counts individual jobs but does not plan a combined route (`backend/src/riders/rider-assignment.service.ts:424–550`; `backend/src/riders/rider-offers.service.ts:567–698`). | Commit direct assignment with the slot reservation and publish after commit. Keep attempt history and idempotent retries. Update rider clients to display assignments without offer actions. To batch, evaluate insertion against existing pickup/drop order, readiness, remaining capacity and SLA before committing. Multiple deliveries sharing rider ID alone do not establish a batch. |
| 5 / High | There is no geographic policy snapshot/activation path for active deliveries in the order and delivery schemas (`backend/src/database/schema/orders.ts:19–58`; `backend/src/database/schema/deliveries.ts:82–109`). | Version settings and make activation apply predictably. Preserve accepted order eligibility and persisted finance inputs; establish an explicit rule for waiting and reassigned work. Avoid silently cancelling, rerouting or repricing an active order when a limit changes. Verify checkout across activation and reassignment across a policy change before rollout. |

Implement activation semantics and snapshot support with the policy in step 3, before switching eligibility or direct assignment on. Existing offer/claim clients must be handled during rollout so old retries cannot bypass the shared capacity guard.

### Business clarification
The requested direction is direct assignment, multi-order batching, atomic capacity and admin-managed road limits. Confirm the service-area shape and hierarchy (global, zone, outlet), who can publish and when, separate distance thresholds and routing vehicle profile, routing-provider outage policy, and whether road distance affects pay. Confirm slot limits and mixed-restaurant rules, pickup sequencing, detour and lateness limits, rider opt-out or no-response handling, and what counts as an active order for grandfathering (cart, paid, kitchen accepted, assigned or picked up). Confirm whether retries use the original or current policy.
